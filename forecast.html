Sap forecast calculator

Will tomorrow run hard, stay quiet, or sit somewhere in between?

This tool reads the next seven days of forecast data for Embrun and scores each day for maple sap potential. It uses freeze/thaw timing, daytime warmth, sunshine, moisture, wind, and seasonal timing to build a practical field-style outlook.

What the score means

It is a forecast index, not a promise.

Trees, taps, slope, tubing, and woods management still matter. The score is meant to answer the question that comes up every spring: is it worth gearing up tomorrow?

    Forecast logic

    Research assumptions behind the calculator.

    • Best sap runs usually come with below-freezing nights and daytime highs in roughly the high 30s to mid-40s F.
    • Repeated freeze/thaw cycles matter more than a single warm day.
    • Sunshine can help warm trees, while very strong wind and warm nights can reduce the run.
    • Moisture and seasonal timing help, but field conditions still win over any browser model.
