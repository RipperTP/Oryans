Sap to syrup math

Two useful rules behind the numbers.

Quick rule

About 40 liters of sap for 1 liter of syrup.

That classic 40:1 rule gives a fast back-of-the-envelope estimate for sugar maple sap.

More precise rule

The 86 rule adjusts for sugar content.

If sap sugar averages 2.2%, one liter of syrup takes roughly 86 / 2.2 liters of sap, or about 39 liters.
